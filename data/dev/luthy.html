Assistant Professor

Phone: 336.702.1964

Office: 455 Vine Street, Bldg 60 South, Room 4510

Email Me

Education

Ph.D. Computer Engineering, North Carolina State University
M.S. Computer Engineering, North Carolina State University
B.S. Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Computer Science, Louisiana State University

Research

The Luthy Lab investigates field-deployable unmanned distributed sensor systems and robotics.  These systems are currently focused on the long-term monitoring of maritime environments as well as distributed sensing of fires throughout the atmospheric boundary layer.
